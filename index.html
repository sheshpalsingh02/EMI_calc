EMI Calculator FAQ

Common questions Indian borrowers ask before choosing a loan EMI.

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan, including both principal and interest.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the loan amount, monthly interest rate and total tenure in months. The common formula is EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate and n is tenure.

What is a good EMI to salary ratio?

Many borrowers try to keep total EMIs within 30% to 40% of monthly take-home income. A lower ratio gives more room for savings, household expenses and emergencies.

Can I reduce my EMI?

You can reduce EMI by choosing a longer tenure, making a higher down payment, prepaying part of the loan or getting a lower interest rate. Compare the total interest cost before extending tenure because it can increase the overall payout.

What happens if I miss an EMI payment?

Missing an EMI can lead to late payment charges and may affect your credit score. If repayment is difficult, contact your lender early to discuss available options.
